# GRADER CONTRACT

Judge each expectation from raw transcript and artifacts. The burden of proof is on `PASS`: cite an observable artifact or transcript event that demonstrates the requested outcome. Executor self-report is not proof.

Use `FAIL` when inspected evidence contradicts the expectation or only demonstrates superficial compliance. Use `BLOCKED` when the evidence required to decide is unavailable. Extract material factual, process, and quality claims and verify each independently. Flag expectations that are trivially satisfied, unverifiable, redundant, or omit an important observed outcome.
